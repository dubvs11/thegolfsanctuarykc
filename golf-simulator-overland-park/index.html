Why a simulator

More golf, less time

Weather, daylight, slow play and tee times stop most golfers from playing as often as they want. At The Golf Sanctuary there is no wind, rain, heat or cold, and the club is available 24 hours a day. Two players can finish nine holes in under an hour, and a full 18 takes well under two hours.

Memberships are intentionally limited, so members can book the time they want. It feels like having a private country club in your own basement, only better.

The technology

ProTee VX launch monitors

Every bay uses ProTee VX, a ceiling-mounted, ultra high-speed launch monitor. It records ball speed, spin, launch angle, club path, face angle, carry distance and more, so you get instant, accurate feedback on every swing.

FAQ

Questions about joining

How much does a private golf simulator membership cost in Overland Park?

Membership plans at The Golf Sanctuary range from monthly plans to prepaid hour blocks and yearly access, so you can pick the one that fits how often you play. Current plans and prices are listed on our home page.

Is The Golf Sanctuary open to the public?

No. It is a private, membership-only club. There are no walk-ins, and memberships are limited so members can always book the tee times they want.

What simulator technology do you use?

The Golf Sanctuary uses ProTee VX, a ceiling-mounted, high-speed launch monitor that tracks ball data and club data for every shot.

Which golf courses can I play?

More than 2,400 courses, including Augusta National, Pebble Beach, St. Andrews, Oakmont, Bethpage Black and Whistling Straits.

How long does a round take?

Two players can play nine holes in about 45 to 60 minutes, and a full 18 takes well under two hours, with no waiting on the group ahead.
