The Reasons for Golf Simulators

Need an excuse to golf twice a week?

If you are reading this, I'm guessing you would like to play more golf. Wouldn't we all like to be able to play three or four rounds a week? What the Secret Society of Golf was built for is to make that happen.

Whether you have a true passion for the game or you just like playing 18 with the guys and you're desperate to make that happen more often, a little thing called life always gets in the way of more golf time. The obstacles are almost never ending when it comes to playing 18 holes at a golf course.

The Weather
90% humidity that makes you feel like you're swinging through soup
100° days that drain you before you reach the 5th tee
40° mornings where your hands go numb by the 3rd hole
30 mph winds turning your perfect drive into a sideways adventure
Rain that cancels your round — again
A saturated course still closed two days after that rain
The Logistics
Carving 4–6 hours out of an already packed weekend
Sneaking away from work for an entire afternoon — not happening
Finding a friend whose schedule actually lines up with yours
Daylight — the most undefeated opponent in golf
Heck, just finding an open tee time
The Costs
Getting paired with a stranger who takes 3 practice swings per shot
S L O W   P L A Y. The silent killer of every round
Green fees that make you question all of your life choices
  • At The Golf Sanctuary, we have alleviated all of those issues. There is no wind, no rain, no humidity, no heat, no cold, just 24 hours of golf time every single day.
  • Do you do air swings while at work just thinking of playing? Now you can have a business meeting while playing Pebble Beach in the middle of the day. Two people can play 9 holes in 45–60 minutes. Boom, you've now impressed your colleague while actually working!
  • If you're like me, and always want to play the full 18, daylight is a huge problem. There's nothing worse than rushing on the back nine because the sun is almost down. Great, you paid for 18 and only got 14 holes in and the last 4 you played were terrible because you were in a massive hurry.
  • I'll bet you've been sitting on your couch when you get the urge to play, but it's already 8pm. No problem at The Golf Sanctuary. You could be on the #1 tee box at St. Andrews 10 minutes after you got the itch and still be home in time to say goodnight to the kiddos.
  • Is your weekend packed with other things to do besides waiting on bad players in front of you for 5–6 hours? Can't get a weekend tee time to save your life? No problem here at The Golf Sanctuary. When it only takes less than an hour to play a full 18 holes, and 24 available hours of golf time in a day, tee times are not an issue.
  • Just imagine how many rounds you HAVEN'T played when you wanted to because of the litany of obstacles that come with outside golf. Life is too short to not play at least 50 rounds of golf a year. Let's turn the number of rounds you play in a year from 10–20 into 156. Or more!!!
  • Oh, and to top it all off, you can play Augusta National, Pebble, Oakmont, Beth Page Black, Whistling Straits, St. Andrews, etc… Did I mention you could play Augusta National?!?!
